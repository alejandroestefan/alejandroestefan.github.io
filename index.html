I study how economic policy and the private sector shape economic development. My work combines field experiments, administrative data, and structural economic models to examine state capacity, firm productivity, labor markets, and economic mobility. Although the questions are global, much of my empirical work is grounded in partnerships with governments and private-sector organizations across Latin America.

Cross-cutting theme

Gender

A consistent finding across my published work is that intervention effects differ sharply by gender: digital delivery can eliminate participation penalties for women, while security technologies disproportionately benefit female entrepreneurs. Ongoing projects in Bolivia, Guatemala, and Paraguay examine this margin directly.
